# CoachSlot — Product brief

## Problem

Many small gyms manage personal training sessions through messages,
phone calls, or paper. This causes real problems:

- Two clients sometimes show up for the same session with the same coach.
- When a coach cancels a session, clients often find out only when they arrive.
- Coaches don't have one clear view of their upcoming sessions.
- The owner spends hours answering "is there a free slot tomorrow?"
  messages and fixing scheduling mistakes by hand.

CoachSlot lets clients book 1-to-1 sessions with a coach online, while
the gym and its coaches always see the same accurate schedule.

## Users

### Owner

- **Who they are:** The person who runs the gym.
- **What they need:** A clear overview of all coaches, sessions, and
  bookings, and control over who works at the gym.
- **What frustrates them today:** Keeping track of everything by hand
  takes a lot of time and leads to mistakes.

### Coach

- **Who they are:** Personal trainers who work at the gym.
- **What they need:** To set when they are available, see their
  upcoming sessions, and know which client is coming and when.
- **What frustrates them today:** Their schedule is spread across
  messages and notes, so double-bookings and missed sessions happen.

### Client

- **Who they are:** People who book personal training sessions at the gym.
- **What they need:** To see when a coach is free, book a session
  themselves, and cancel if their plans change.
- **What frustrates them today:** They have to message and wait for
  an answer to book, and they may not hear about cancellations in time.

## What v1 does

### Owner

- Creates the gym account.
- Invites coaches and removes them.
- Defines session types (for example "60-min personal training").
- Sees all bookings for all coaches.

### Coach

- Sets weekly working hours and marks days off.
- Sees their upcoming sessions in a weekly schedule.
- Cancels a session or marks a client as a no-show.

### Client

- Creates an account and logs in.
- Chooses a session type and a coach, and sees available time slots.
- Books an available slot.
- Sees their upcoming and past bookings.
- Cancels a booking up to 24 hours before it starts.

## Not in v1

- Payments
- Workout plans and progress tracking
- Group classes
- SMS notifications
- Mobile app
- Real-time updates

## Success looks like

- A client can book a session on their phone in under 1 minute.
- The same slot can never be booked twice.
- A coach can see all of their sessions for the week on one screen.
